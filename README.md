**Overview**
Boomi's Earth Adventure is a browser-based educational platformer built for middle-school-aged players (ages 11–12). Players guide Boomi, a young environmental hero, through four themed worlds — each tackling a real environmental issue — collecting clean-energy tokens while dodging pollution-based hazards. It's designed so that learning happens through gameplay, not around it: every enemy, token, and level fact ties directly to a real-world environmental concept.

**The Four Stages**
Air Pollution Crisis — dodge smog clouds and car exhaust, collect solar panels
The Wasteland — navigate landfill trash and toxic blobs, collect recyclables
Coal vs. Clean Energy — outrun coal miners and oil spills, collect wind/hydro tokens
Saving the Planet — survive rising heat waves and falling ice, collect climate-action tokens
Each stage opens with a short, age-appropriate intro explaining the environmental theme, and closes with a real fact tied to what the player just experienced (e.g., "Recycling aluminum saves 95% of the energy needed to make new aluminum from raw materials").

**Gameplay**
Classic side-scrolling platforming: run, jump, collect, avoid
5 lives per playthrough, with a forgiving checkpoint-respawn system
Difficulty ramps gradually — Stage 1 is gentle for beginners, Stage 4 is the real challenge
Token collection unlocks each stage's exit, reinforcing the idea that clean-energy choices "power the way forward"
Full HUD (lives, token progress, stage name) and stage-complete summary screens reinforcing what was learned
Why It Works for the Audience
The game deliberately avoids punishing failure — plenty of lives, slow early hazards, and safe respawns — so kids stay engaged and keep playing rather than getting stuck or frustrated. The environmental facts are delivered at natural pause points (between stages) rather than interrupting gameplay, so the "lesson" never feels like a lecture.

**Tech**
Built entirely in vanilla HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript — a single self-contained file, no frameworks, no backend, no install. Runs instantly in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and is hosted live via GitHub Pages, making it trivially shareable for judging.
